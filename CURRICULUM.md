# Curriculum outline (EN / ES)

1. Wallet setup: choosing and installing a self-custody wallet
2. Seed phrase: backing it up and protecting it
3. Verifying addresses before you send
4. Receiving money from family abroad
5. Lightning basics
6. Recognizing scam scripts: urgency, fear, authority, secrecy
7. Signet scenarios: fake support chat, fake "recovery service", address poisoning, lookalike wallet site
8. Pressure scenarios: romance and investment setups, family-emergency impersonation
9. Educator guide and translation guide

Planned over 6 months; all work is public from the first commit.
